At Cattitude, we are committed to making a difference. Approximately
30 percent of every purchase you make is dedicated to supporting our
local stray cat shelter. Each sip of your coffee contributes to offering
these cherished creatures the warmth and care they deserve, helping
them find their way to a loving home.
